💡 Training Tips
  • 80/20 rule: Run 80% of your weekly mileage at an easy, conversational pace.
  • Rest days: Recovery is when your body gets stronger — never skip a rest day.
  • 10% rule: Increase your weekly mileage by no more than 10% per week to prevent injury.
  • Hydration: Drink 500 ml of water 2 hours before a long run, and sip during.
